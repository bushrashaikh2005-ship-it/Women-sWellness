Self-care for PCOS is not about fixing

It’s about listening. Supporting your hormones gently, without punishment or extremes.

Gentle Self-Care Practices

🌿 Nourish Slowly

Balanced meals, enough protein, and regular eating help stabilize energy and hormones.

🚶‍♀️ Soft Movement

Walking, stretching, yoga, or slow workouts support insulin balance without stress.

🧘 Nervous System Care

Breathing, journaling, prayer, or quiet moments help lower cortisol levels.

🌙 Prioritize Rest

Consistent sleep routines support hormone regulation and emotional balance.

🤍 Emotional Boundaries

Doing less, saying no, and resting without guilt is also self-care.

✨ Self-Compassion

You don’t need to be perfect to heal. Gentle consistency matters more.

🌸 Your body is not against you. It’s communicating. Listen kindly.
