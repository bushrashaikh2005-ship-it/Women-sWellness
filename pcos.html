What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal condition that can affect periods, energy levels, emotions, and overall well-being.

PCOS looks different for everyone. You are not broken — your body simply needs gentler care and understanding.

Common Experiences

  • Irregular or missed periods
  • Fatigue or low energy
  • Mood swings or anxiety
  • Weight changes
  • Hormonal acne or hair changes

Gentle Self-Care for PCOS

🌿 Slow Routines

Small, consistent habits matter more than extreme changes.

🧘 Emotional Care

Breathing, journaling, and rest help regulate stress hormones.

🌙 Sleep & Compassion

Sleep and self-kindness are essential for hormonal balance.
